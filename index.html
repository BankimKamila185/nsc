Netaji Subhas Chandra Welfare Sangha (NSCWS) | 20th Malad Durga Puja 2026

Welcome to the official portal of Netaji Subhas Chandra Welfare Sangha (NSCWS), celebrating 20 landmark years of Sarbojanin Malad Durga Puja and Kojagari Lakshmi Puja in Anand Wadi, Kurar Village, Malad East, Mumbai - 400097. Experience sacred Vedic rituals, authentic Ekchala Daaker Saaj pratima darshan, daily Pushpanjali, 108-lamp Sandhi Puja, and free Maha Bhandara bhog prasad distribution.

20th Malad Durga Puja 2026 Schedule & Timetable

  • Maha Panchami: Thursday, October 15, 2026 - Bilva Nimantran & Kalparambha
  • Maha Sasthi: Friday, October 16, 2026 - Bodhon, Amantran & Adhivas
  • Maha Saptami: Saturday, October 17, 2026 - Nabapatrika Pravesh & Pushpanjali (10:30 AM)
  • Maha Ashtami: Sunday, October 18, 2026 - Kumari Puja & Sacred Sandhi Puja (108 Lamps)
  • Maha Nabami: Monday, October 19, 2026 - Nabami Homa & Maha Bhandara Anna Bhog (1:30 PM)
  • Bijoya Dashami: Tuesday, October 20, 2026 - Aparajita Puja, Sindoor Khela & Dhunuchi Naach
  • Kojagari Lakshmi Puja: Saturday, October 24, 2026 - Midnight Vedic Lakshmi Aradhana

Mandap Location & How to Reach

Address: Anand Wadi Durga Puja Mandap Ground, Ganesh Niwas Mitra Mandal, Near B.M.C. Playground, Kurar Village, Malad East, Mumbai - 400097, Maharashtra.

Directions: 10 minutes from Malad Railway Station (East) via BEST Bus 345/347 or auto-rickshaw. Directly accessible via Western Express Highway (Kurar flyover). Entry and Bhog prasad are 100% free for all devotees.
